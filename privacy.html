Privacy policy

Prism for iPhone & iPad · Effective October 25, 2026

Your files stay under your control

Prism does not operate a file-upload service and does not send your documents, media, filenames, folder paths, drafts or file contents to the developer. Viewing, editing and conversion happen on your device. No advertising or third-party analytics SDK is included. You do not create an account with us.

Data on your device

Imported files, exports, draft edits, favorites, recents, folder-access bookmarks, appearance preferences and the count of successful free exports are stored locally. Your device’s backup settings may include app data. Connected cloud folders and destinations you choose in Share are handled by their respective providers.

Purchases

Apple processes payments and supplies signed purchase information through StoreKit. Prism verifies access on your device and does not send purchase transactions to a developer server. Apple handles payment details, refunds and Apple Account information under its own privacy policy. Restore Purchases asks Apple to synchronize purchases only when you choose it.

Permissions and sharing

File access is limited to the files and folders you select. Photos add-only access is used when you choose Save Image or Save Video; Prism does not request permission to read your photo library. Nothing is shared to another app or provider until you choose a sharing action.

This website and support

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives normal web requests and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes under its Privacy Statement. These pages do not add analytics, advertising trackers, cookies or forms. If you contact support, your email service and the support provider process the message and information you choose to include. Apple may separately collect diagnostics according to your device and App Store settings.

Privacy questions and support

Control your data

You can remove imports, exports, favorites and connected folders within Prism. Deleting the app removes its local app container according to iOS. External originals and copies shared to other apps or providers remain under those providers’ controls. Purchases stay associated with your Apple Account.
