Terms & purchases

Prism Pro

Prism Pro removes the count limit on supported edited exports and conversions. Choose a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription, or a non-consumable lifetime purchase. All options include the same Pro features and future Pro updates. Subscription access lasts while your subscription is active. Lifetime is one payment with no recurring charge.

Viewing, playback and file management remain free. Three successful premium exports are included per installation; failed or cancelled jobs do not count. Format, codec, size, device and permission limits remain applicable after purchase. New formats and features become available as released; no specific future feature or delivery date is guaranteed.

Renewal, cancellation and restoration

Full localized prices and billing periods appear in the app before purchase. Apple charges your Apple Account at confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Apple charges for renewal within the 24 hours before that period ends. Manage or cancel in your App Store account settings. Cancellation preserves access until the paid period ends. Purchasing lifetime access does not cancel an existing subscription.

A purchase is associated with your Apple Account. Restore Purchases restores eligible access on compatible iPhone and iPad devices. Apple handles approval, payments and refunds.

Corresponding media-engine source, notices, rights and a binary relinking kit are available offline in Prism under Help & privacy → Libraries & licenses.
